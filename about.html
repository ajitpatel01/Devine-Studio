How we work

Three rules we don’t bend

01

Senior only

No juniors, no subcontractors, no account managers. The people on the call are the people designing the screens and writing the code.

02

Fixed scope

Before you pay, we write down what we will build, by when, and for how much. A change gets its own line in the scope, never a surprise invoice.

03

Honest timelines

We take on a few projects at a time, and we plan for that. If a date is at risk, you hear it from us first.
