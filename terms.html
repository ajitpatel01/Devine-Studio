Terms of Service

The short version of how we work together. Every project also has a written scope, which takes precedence over this page where they differ.

Scope and price

Each project starts with a written scope that sets out the deliverables, the timeline and a fixed price. Work outside the scope is quoted separately and only starts once you approve it in writing.

Payment

We invoice in USD, GBP or EUR. Projects are billed 50% up front to book the slot and 50% at launch. Care and Care Plus are billed monthly. Custom systems begin with a paid discovery, quoted on its own.

Ownership

Once a project is paid in full, you own what we made for you: the code, the designs, the films and the source files. Until then, we keep the rights to the unpaid work. Open-source libraries and third-party tools stay under their own licenses.

Hosting and accounts

Domains, hosting, analytics, AI model and other service accounts are set up in your company’s name and paid by you directly, so you are never dependent on us for access.

30-day warranty

If anything we built stops working as specified within 30 days of launch, we fix it at no charge. The warranty doesn’t cover changes made by others, third-party outages, or new features. Ongoing support is available through Care and Care Plus.

Confidentiality

We keep your non-public information confidential and use it only for your project. We only show your project in our portfolio with your permission.

Liability

We work carefully and stand behind our work, but our total liability for any project is limited to the fees you paid for that project.
