Privacy Policy

We collect as little as we can, use it only to talk to you about your project, and never sell it.

What we collect

When you email us or book a call, we receive what you choose to send: usually your name, work email, and details about your project.

Booking an intro call happens on Cal.com. This website doesn’t store that information.

How we use it

We use your details to reply to you, to scope and deliver your project, and to send invoices. We don’t add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

What we never do

We don’t sell, rent or trade your personal data, and we don’t share it with anyone except the services we need to run the studio (email, calendar booking and invoicing), and only for that purpose.

Client data in projects

When a project involves your customers’ data, it stays in accounts owned by your company. We access it only as needed to build and support what you hired us for, and we remove our access when you ask or when the engagement ends.

Third-party services

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a scrolling library from a public CDN. Booking an intro call uses Cal.com, which has its own privacy policy.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time what we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it. Email us and we will reply within 48 hours.

Questions? Email contact@devinestudio.com.
